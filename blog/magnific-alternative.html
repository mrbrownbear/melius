The best Magnific alternative for upscaling and creative AI

Alex Chen, Member of Technical Staff
Alex Chen, Member of Technical Staff
What Magnific does wellThe frictionThe Melius approachWho should switchFAQ

Magnific made its name as a creative upscaler, and it is still one of the best at it: push an image through and it doesn't just enlarge, it invents plausible new detail, texture and lighting, with sliders to dial how much it reimagines versus preserves.

It is no longer only an upscaler, though. Backed by Freepik's platform it has grown into a genuine node canvas with an in-canvas agent, a timeline video editor and model training, which makes it one of the closest tools to Melius in shape. So a Magnific alternative comparison can't lean on "we have a canvas and an agent" either. The honest differences are how far the agent reaches, how a team works on it at once, and how it plugs into your own stack. Here's a fair look.

What Magnific does well

Magnific's creative upscaling is best-in-class at inventing detail, and its Mystic model, Relight tools and model training give real control over texture, lighting and consistency. Beyond that it now runs a live node canvas with multiplayer, an agent that wires and runs nodes, a timeline video editor and a huge stock library. It is a capable, well-funded platform, not a one-trick upscaler, and worth taking seriously.

Where teams hit friction

  • The agent and MCP stop at generation. Its MCP and integrations are described as generating assets rather than building or running the canvas remotely, and there's no native Slack workflow, so an external agent can make images but can't operate the canvas end to end for you.
  • Deterministic flows, not team skills. Customization runs through preset flows rather than natural-language, team-defined slash-command skills, so teaching the agent your brand voice as reusable commands isn't really the model.
  • Detail that drifts. The same hallucination that makes the upscaler impressive can "overcook" an image, inventing detail that diverges from the original, especially on photorealistic portraits.
  • Premium pricing, no free trial. Users report no free tier to test with and a strict no-refund policy, so evaluating it means paying up front, and heavy trial-and-error burns credits.

How Melius approaches the same work

Melius and Magnific overlap more than most pairs in this list, so the differences are about how far the agent reaches, not whether one exists.

  • An agent that reaches past the canvas. Mel plans and runs multi-step work on the canvas and also inside Slack, so a request, an approval and a finished download can happen in the thread where briefs actually arrive, not only in the app.
  • A canvas-level MCP. An external agent like Claude can build and run the Melius canvas itself, creating nodes, wiring them and executing, rather than only calling a generation endpoint. That's the difference between an agent that operates your workspace and one that just returns assets.
  • Teach it your brand, run many at once. Give the agent brand instructions plus reference images and save custom slash-command skills, then run several agents concurrently with visible presence on the canvas, personalization and a workforce, not a single assistant.
  • Resize and enhance in-line, one subscription. Magic resize, image enhancement and background removal live as nodes, so an upscale flows straight into the next step, and every image, video and audio model shares one subscription with up to 8 seats on self-serve plans, unlimited seats on Enterprise, and unlimited agent usage.
  • Your own work, semantically searchable. Past Magnific's stock library, every image you generate or upload is auto-indexed by content, so you find your own past work by describing it rather than remembering a filename or re-upscaling from scratch.

Who should switch

If your job is squeezing maximum detail out of a single image, or you want the deepest timeline video editing, Magnific is a strong, well-funded choice and worth keeping in the toolbox. If the bottleneck is throughput across a team, an agent that runs the workflow in Slack and on the canvas, is drivable from your own AI tools, and learns your brand as reusable skills, Melius reaches further with up to 8 seats on self-serve plans, unlimited seats on Enterprise, and unlimited agent usage.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Magnific alternative?

Melius is the strongest alternative for teams. Magnific is a capable, well-funded platform, a real node canvas with an in-canvas agent, a timeline editor and model training on top of its best-in-class upscaler, so this isn't a canvas-versus-no-canvas comparison. The wedge is agent reach: on Melius the agent also runs inside Slack, the MCP is canvas-level so an external agent can build and run the canvas itself, teams teach it their brand as reusable slash-command skills, and self-serve plans include up to 8 seats, Enterprise includes unlimited seats, and agent usage is unlimited.

Does Melius upscale and resize images like Magnific?

Yes. Melius includes magic resize and image enhancement as steps on the canvas, alongside background removal and other edits. The difference is that these live inside an end-to-end workflow: generate, edit, resize and hand off, rather than a standalone upscaler you round-trip through.

Why do people look for a Magnific alternative?

Commonly cited reasons: it is priced at the premium end with no free trial and a strict no-refund policy, its creative upscaling can "overcook" images and invent detail that drifts from the original, and heavy trial-and-error burns credits. Its agent and MCP are also generation-focused rather than driving the canvas remotely, and there is no native Slack workflow, so teams that want an agent reaching across their whole stack look elsewhere.

How does Magnific AI work?

Magnific is a creative upscaler: you feed it an image and it enlarges it while inventing plausible new detail, texture and lighting, with sliders to control how much it reimagines versus preserves the original. That hallucination is its strength and its risk, which is why control over how much it changes the image matters. In Melius, upscaling and enhancement are connected nodes in a larger pipeline rather than a standalone round trip.

Is Magnific's upscaler better than Melius?

Magnific's creative upscaler is genuinely strong at inventing plausible detail, and for pure maximum-detail upscaling it is a specialist worth using. Melius optimizes for the whole creative pipeline instead of one step, so the question is less which upscaler wins and more whether you want a standalone enhancer or a workspace where enhancement is one connected node.

Does Melius have a canvas?

Yes. Melius is built canvas-first: a node-based canvas where prompts, images, video and audio are connected nodes, with Mel to plan and wire the work and real-time multiplayer for teams.
