What is Grok Imagine Video?

Grok Imagine Video is xAI's fast video model for generating and restyling video from a text prompt, an image, or existing footage.

How do I use Grok Imagine Video on Melius?

Melius is a node-based canvas: add a video node, pick Grok Imagine Video from the model picker, and connect it to whatever should feed it or follow it. You only pay for what you generate.
